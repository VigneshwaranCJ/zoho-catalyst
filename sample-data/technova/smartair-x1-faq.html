SmartAir X1 Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about the SmartAir X1 smart air purifier.

Product

Does the SmartAir X1 support Wi-Fi?

Yes. It connects to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks (802.11 b/g/n). 5 GHz networks are not supported.

How often should I replace the filter?

Every 6 months under normal use. The app and the filter indicator tell you when it is time.

Can I use the SmartAir X1 without the app?

Yes. All modes can be controlled with the buttons on the top panel. The app is only needed for schedules, remote control and air-quality history.

Is the filter washable?

Only the pre-filter can be vacuumed or rinsed. The HEPA and carbon layers must not be washed.

Where can I buy replacement filters?

From the TechNova online store or authorised resellers (part number TN-SAX1-F).

Warranty and returns

How long is the warranty?

The SmartAir X1 has a 24-month limited warranty from the date of purchase.

Can I return the product?

Yes, within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, with all original accessories and packaging.
